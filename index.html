A little story about me

When I got my driver's license, I was scared to drive because I was not confident in my driving skills. My dad saw that, and he told me something when I was stopped at a red light:

How much petrol you burn is equal to how good your driving skill is! So drive more!

Like any other dad, he taught me many life lessons, but this is the only one I remember. It turned out to be the one I use at work, too: the first pipeline, query or dashboard is never the last one. You build it, watch it run, and rebuild it better. Competence is mostly mileage: in a car, or in a data warehouse.

What I do

I came to data from the business side, with extensive experience across financial advisory, immigration consulting and e-commerce. That work taught me what a stakeholder actually needs from a report. Now I leverage the technical skills I have built since to support business decisions backed by data, from ingestion and medallion-architecture warehouses to tested transformations and semantic models that hold up when someone checks the numbers.

The clearest highlight of that transition came as a research assistant at Langara College, where I built the data layer behind a community-services app for shelter staff. That work contributed to a project later awarded a $359,928 NSERC grant.

The financial-market analytics platform is a different project, and one I started on my own after the Langara program to teach myself data engineering. I built it first in dbt, Airflow and Streamlit on Google Cloud. After earning the Fabric Analytics Engineer certification, I ported the whole pipeline to Microsoft Fabric and rebuilt the reporting in Power BI. I then added a Tableau Public dashboard fed by Google Sheets through Apps Script. Both versions are still running.

Financial market analytics platform

Microsoft Fabric · Power BI · Pyspark · Google Cloud · Airflow · dbt · Streamlit

An end-to-end ELT platform ingesting daily prices, company profiles and news sentiment for the three most actively traded stocks. Built twice: a Bronze–Silver–Gold medallion architecture of four PySpark notebooks in Microsoft Fabric, and the original open-source stack on GCP.

  • Developed a financial market analytics platform to analyze the day’s three most actively traded stocks, identify potentialdrivers of sharp price and volume movements, and present market data through Power BI.
  • Extracted and transformed raw API data into analysis-ready Delta tables using PySpark and a medallion architecture in Microsoft Fabric, applying data cleaning, validation, and aggregation.
  • Designed a Power BI semantic model and DAX measures comparing closing prices and trading volumes with 100-day averages to highlight unusual activity and support analysis of market trends.
  • Built and deployed a GCP version of the platform, running Airflow and dbt in Docker on a virtual machine for daily incremental PostgreSQL updates and hosting a Streamlit dashboard on Cloud Run.

AI-powered HVAC optimisation

Python · sklearn · Tableau · beautifulsoup

Capstone for Langara College's facilities department and Prism Engineering: detecting anomalies in HVAC sensor data and forecasting temperature 15 minutes ahead to support energy-efficient control.

  • Analyzed real-time HVAC sensor data from five air-handling units serving 117 zones in Langara College’s Building B, using Python to identify operational anomalies and energy-efficiency opportunities.
  • Developed a CNN-LSTM model to forecast 15-minute zone-temperature changes and support data-driven HVAC set-point recommendations that balance energy efficiency with occupant comfort.
  • Translated analytical and model outputs into anomaly heat maps and an interactive Tableau dashboard, presenting decision-ready findings to Langara Facilities and Prism Engineering in support of operational improvements.
  • Won the Innovative Award at Langara's Applied Research Day 2025.
